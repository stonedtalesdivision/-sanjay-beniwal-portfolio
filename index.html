TECHNICAL CASE STUDY

AMRIT: 187

The project is designed around a smartphone-first third-person combat loop where the player can switch between two active characters while an AI partner supports the player.

Character switchingPlayer and AI control states transfer without loading.
Partner AIFollow, combat, protect and revive behaviours using a Behaviour Tree / Blackboard architecture.
Companion AISugar has distinct follow, detect, warn, weak-target attack and strong-target retreat states.
Production disciplinePrototype the core interaction before investing in a huge world or final content.

Status: pre-production / prototype planning. The Unreal implementation is not presented as completed production work.
